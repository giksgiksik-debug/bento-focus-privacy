Bento Focus

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Bento Focus handles information when you use the Android app.

Who operates Bento Focus

Bento Focus is published by Giksik. For privacy questions, contact giksgiksik@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Bento Focus is a focus timer. Depending on how you use it, the app stores timer preferences, sound and automation settings, focus-session statistics, tags, and text you enter for tasks or notes in the app's private local storage.

Timer settings, tags, focus-session statistics, and task or note text are stored locally on your device. Bento Focus does not send this focus content to a developer-operated server and does not provide developer-operated user accounts. The app's dependencies do not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Device sensors

When Flip to Focus is enabled and the app is active, it uses the device's accelerometer and, where available, proximity and ambient-light sensors to detect when the phone is face down on a surface. Sensor readings are processed on the device to detect the gesture and are not sent to a developer-operated server.

Android backup is enabled in the app configuration. Depending on your Android device and backup settings, some app data may be included in Android backup and restored to a device. Bento Focus does not control the operation of Android's backup service.

Permissions and device features

  • Notifications: used for timer and session notifications.
  • Exact alarms: used to schedule timer phase transitions as accurately as Android permits, subject to device settings and permissions.
  • Do Not Disturb access: if you enable DND Sync and grant system access, the app can change the system's Do Not Disturb state during focus and restore the prior state when focus is paused or ends.
  • Vibration and foreground services: used for timer alerts and to keep an active timer, and user-selected ambience audio, operating while the app is in the background.
  • Start after reboot: the app may use Android's boot broadcast to restore timer service behavior when appropriate.

You can manage app permissions in Android system settings. Disabling a permission or restricting background activity may limit the corresponding feature.

One-time Pro purchase

Bento Focus offers an optional one-time Pro purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the transaction. The app checks the product's displayed price and purchase state through Google Play so it can unlock or restore the Pro entitlement. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. The reviewed implementation does not send purchase data to a developer-operated server.

Purchase history remains subject to Google's own terms and privacy disclosures.

Sharing and advertising

Bento Focus does not display third-party advertising. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Google Play Billing and Android system services operate under their own terms and privacy information.

Retention and deletion

Focus content and settings remain in the app's local storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android system backups, if enabled, may retain backed-up app data according to your device and account backup settings. A Pro purchase is managed by Google Play and may be restored through the same Google account used to make the purchase.

Bento Focus does not currently provide user accounts, so it does not hold developer-server account data for an account-deletion workflow.

Children's privacy

Bento Focus is not designed specifically for children. The developer must accurately complete the Google Play target-audience and content declarations before publishing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, dependencies, or data handling change. The current version should be available at the public URL entered in Google Play Console and accessible from within the app.
